COMPACT ARTICLE WITH INTEGRATED APPENDICES

The only document root is wileyNJD-APA.tex. It includes appendices.tex through
Wiley's native appendix environment before one shared bibliography. Compile once
with scripts/build_manuscript_review.py --output-dir FRESH_DIRECTORY, or validate
and build through scripts/publication_package.py --build-dir FRESH_DIRECTORY.
The complete article is one PDF; no separate supplement is submitted.

Appendix A describes data sources and historical inputs. Appendix B reports
origin-specific scores, component comparisons, fitting evidence, quantile losses,
score/scale sensitivity, calibration and coefficient summaries. Appendix C gives
state-space construction and computational details. Appendix D contains the two
variational Bayes algorithms. All references are ordinary local LaTeX labels;
lettered exhibit numbers and hyperlink destinations are checked in the proof.

PUBLICATION_PACKAGE.json inventories actual included assets and their logical
main-body/appendix scopes. Main-only obligations cannot be satisfied solely by
appendix text. Unused retained files cannot count as submitted evidence.

Original results, data, source tables/figures, model code and settings are preserved.
scripts/compact_publication.py regenerates only compact presentations from saved
full-precision summaries; --check is read-only. The calibration note now links to
the appendix's numerical quantile conventions. Its numerical cells are unchanged.
All eleven tables, seven figures and both retained algorithms remain submitted.

The compact article keeps six scientific main sections and the model equations,
with detailed evidence and computation in appendices. Optional displays omitted
in the preceding editorial revision remain in Git. Historical numerical replay
and the 15-file/1496-number presentation proof remain intact.
